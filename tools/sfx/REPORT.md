# Fonia sound effects v2: measured report

Generated by `tools/sfx/verify.py` from the shipped OGG files (`legacy/design/src/main/assets/sfx/`). Rebuild with `tools/sfx/build.py` (candidates are scored by `tools/sfx/audition.py` into `audition.json`), then re-run this. Nobody has listened: every statement is a measurement or an earshot reading.

v2 replaces the v1 set the owner rejected (thin synthesised beeps). Direction: a top children's learning game, juicy, bubbly, sparkling; sources in order ElevenLabs sound effects, earshot's bank, computed layers only underneath.

- **Family**: C major (every tonal sound key-fitted to the white keys); ElevenLabs mallets, kalimba, glockenspiel, harp, bubbles and real-sounding parrot calls, earshot-bank wood / glock / shimmer; never a falling or buzzing cue.
- **Format**: OGG Vorbis q4, 48 kHz; stereo for the rewards (side narrowed to 0.8 + a computed room), mono for UI ticks and Foni; total **280 KB** for 24 files (gate 900 KB).
- **Levels**: max momentary loudness per tier (LUFS-M, BS.1770, 400 ms windows; ours steps 10 ms, ffmpeg 100 ms): strong -17.0, medium -20.0, soft -23.0, subtle -26.0; his voice clips sit near -15 LUFS, so every effect is 2-11 dB under the voice. True-peak ceiling -3 dBTP before the codec (gate <= -1 after).
- **Chain**: key fit (resample, <= 1.5 semitones), high-pass 110-400 Hz, low-pass 7-14 kHz, tanh saturation 10-25 % (warmth), computed stereo room 0.25-1.2 s at 4-12 % wet, trim (onset -1 ms, -60 dB tail), fades, tier gain with a 4 dB look-ahead limiter.
- **Key**: each tonal sound is resampled by the shift (<= 150 cents) that best lands its strongest partials on C major's white keys (`key` column: shift / remaining weighted error in cents; Foni's glides and the whooshes are not tonal). The two tap variants and the retake are transposed and re-landed on white keys.
- **ElevenLabs**: 7 generations in v1, 49 in v2; every raw file is kept in `tools/sfx/raw/elevenlabs/` (v2 in `v2/`), every v2 candidate's earshot reading in `audition.json`.

## Playback and the quiet rule (unchanged from v1)

`SfxGate` (legacy/design) is the one place that decides: nothing starts while a take captures; `AudioHub.startListening` waits until the last effect has ended + 150 ms (60 ms latency allowed); in the 150 ms after the capture closes an outcome is deferred, a UI sound dropped; two effects closer than 120 ms never stack; the in-app switch, silent / vibrate ringer and Do Not Disturb silence everything; TalkBack mutes the chrome sounds. `SfxPlayer`: one preloaded `SoundPool` (4 streams, media volume). Taps rotate over three variants, Foni over six calls. His `SoundEffects` player is no longer created (`AppModel`: no effects folder), and `AudioHub.effectsMuted` is FoniaSfx's switch. Proof: `SfxQuietRuleTests` (1,000 random sessions through the real AudioHub, plus merge / deferral / switch / round-robin / asset tests).
